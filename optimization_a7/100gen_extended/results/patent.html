Sequence & structure metrics

Three sequence-identity metrics are reported. They share the same numerator (matched nucleotides from the Smith-Waterman local alignment) but differ in the denominator, which can produce meaningfully different values when query and reference differ in length.

Id / aln len
matches ÷ alignment_length
Standard pairwise metric. Penalises gapped alignments.
Id / shorter ★
matches ÷ min(len_q, len_r)
Best-case from the shorter sequence's perspective. Most commonly referenced in patent oligonucleotide claims.
Id / longer
matches ÷ max(len_q, len_r)
Worst-case. Penalises when one sequence is much longer than the other.

U→T normalisation: All sequences (queries and patent references) are converted to DNA alphabet (U replaced with T) before alignment. This ensures RNA and DNA forms of equivalent sequences are not penalised by spurious U/T mismatches.

Secondary structure (RNAfold): MFE dot-bracket structures are predicted independently for the query and the best-matching patent sequence. Matching secondary structure topology can indicate functional equivalence even when sequence identity is below the threshold — and may be relevant to broader claim interpretation.

Covariance model (cmsearch): If a .cm file is supplied via --cm, Infernal's cmsearch is used to score queries against a probabilistic model of the RNA's sequence and structure simultaneously. Output is written to three persistent files alongside the HTML report. This is the most rigorous test for functional equivalence of non-coding RNAs.
